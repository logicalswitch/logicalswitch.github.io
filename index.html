Services

Electrical installation

Modernising your existing installation or a completely new one, with wired or radio-frequency solutions, carried out in line with the AREI/RGIE, the Belgian regulation for electrical installations.

KNX and Niko Home Control

Control of lighting, shutters and heating with KNX or Niko Home Control, including dimmable LED and DALI lighting with scenes and presence detection, from design to commissioning.

Smart home integration

Home Assistant setup that brings your devices together in one app, running locally in your home.

Software development

Custom applications, websites and integrations that connect your systems, built around the way you work.

IT and computers

Computer repairs, home and office networks, Wi-Fi, security and backups, plus honest advice before you buy.

Custom solutions

We listen to your needs and your budget, and work with you as partners to design a solution that fits your home or business.
